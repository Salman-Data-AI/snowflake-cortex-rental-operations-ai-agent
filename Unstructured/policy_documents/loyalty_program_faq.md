# Avelto Rentals — Loyalty Program FAQ (Avelto Miles)

**Document type:** Customer-facing FAQ
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

**What is Avelto Miles?**
Avelto Miles is our free loyalty program. Members earn 10 points per day rented, regardless of category, plus a 500-point bonus for every one-way rental completed.

**How do I redeem points?**
Points can be redeemed for rental credit ($1 = 100 points) or category upgrades at pickup, subject to availability. Upgrade redemptions cannot be combined with the Young Driver Surcharge waiver.

**What are the membership tiers?**
- **Silver** (0–4,999 points): standard earning rate, priority email support.
- **Gold** (5,000–14,999 points): 1.25x earning rate, free CDW on Economy/Compact/SUV rentals, dedicated phone line.
- **Platinum** (15,000+ points): 1.5x earning rate, free CDW on all categories, guaranteed vehicle availability with 48-hour notice, complimentary one category upgrade per rental.

**Do points expire?**
Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity (no rentals or point redemptions).

**Can I earn points on the new Convertible category?**
Yes. Convertible rentals, currently available at our Adamstad, Andersonshire, and North Valerie branches as part of our fleet pilot, earn points at the standard rate and count toward tier qualification like any other category.

**How do I check my balance?**
Members can check their point balance and tier status by contacting Support with their account email, or through the Avelto Rentals customer portal.

**Can points be transferred between accounts?**
No, Avelto Miles points are non-transferable and tied to the account that earned them.

**What happens to my tier if I don't rent for a while?**
Tier status is reviewed annually based on trailing-12-month rental activity. Members who fall below a tier's qualifying activity are moved down one tier at the annual review, not immediately.
