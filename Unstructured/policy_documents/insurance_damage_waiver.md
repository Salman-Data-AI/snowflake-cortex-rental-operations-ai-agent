# Avelto Rentals — Insurance & Damage Waiver Policy

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Applies to:** All rental categories
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Included Coverage

Every Avelto Rentals booking includes Basic Liability Coverage at no additional cost, covering third-party bodily injury and property damage up to the statutory minimum of the pickup country. Basic Liability Coverage does not cover damage to the rented vehicle itself.

## 2. Collision Damage Waiver (CDW)

Customers may add CDW at checkout for a daily fee that varies by category: Economy/Compact $12/day, SUV $16/day, Luxury/Sports/Convertible $28/day. CDW reduces the customer's financial responsibility for accidental damage to the rented vehicle to a fixed deductible, currently $500 for Economy/Compact/SUV and $1,000 for Luxury/Sports/Convertible.

CDW does not cover: damage from prohibited use as defined in the Rental Agreement Terms, damage to tires or undercarriage from off-road or unpaved-road driving, interior damage from smoking, or loss of personal belongings left in the vehicle.

## 3. Filing a Damage Claim

If damage occurs during the rental period, the customer must report it to Avelto Rentals within 24 hours, either at the return branch or through the Support channel. Filing a claim requires: reservation number, a description of the damage, and photos where possible. Claims for damage present at pickup but not noted on the digital inspection form should reference the original pickup inspection timestamp.

## 4. Roadside and Mechanical Issues

Mechanical failures not caused by customer misuse are covered under the Roadside Assistance Guide at no cost to the customer, including towing to the nearest Avelto branch or partner garage.

## 5. Theft

In the event of theft, the customer must file a police report within 24 hours and provide the report reference number to Avelto Rentals. Customers without CDW are responsible for the vehicle's depreciated value up to a cap of $2,500; customers with CDW are responsible only for the applicable deductible.

## 6. Country-Specific Notes

Minimum liability coverage amounts and required documentation vary by country. See the Driving Rules by Region document for country-specific insurance minimums applicable in the United States, Canada, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Japan, and Australia.
