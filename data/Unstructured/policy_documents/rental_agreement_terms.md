# Avelto Rentals — Standard Rental Agreement Terms

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Applies to:** All rental categories (Economy, Compact, SUV, Luxury, Sports, Convertible)
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## 1. Eligibility

Renters must be at least 21 years old and hold a valid driver's license that has been active for a minimum of 12 months. Renters under 25 renting a Sports or Convertible category vehicle are subject to a Young Driver Surcharge of $25/day. A valid driver's license number and date of birth must be on file before pickup.

## 2. Reservation and Pickup

Reservations are held for two hours past the scheduled pickup time. Vehicles must be picked up and returned at the branch(es) specified at booking. Avelto Rentals supports one-way rentals: a vehicle may be picked up at one branch and returned to a different branch, subject to a One-Way Repositioning Fee that varies by branch pair and category.

At pickup, staff will walk through existing vehicle condition and note it in the digital inspection form. Renters should confirm the fuel level matches the agreement before leaving the lot.

## 3. Rental Period and Daily Rates

Each category carries a base daily rate set centrally in USD: Economy $40, Compact $50, SUV $70, Luxury $120, Sports $200, Convertible $150. Customers are billed in the local currency of the pickup branch's country, converted from the USD list price at the prevailing rate at time of booking. Rentals are billed in full-day increments; returns more than 59 minutes past the agreed return time are billed as an additional full day.

## 4. Fuel Policy

Vehicles are provided with a full tank and must be returned full. Vehicles returned with less than a full tank are charged a refueling service fee plus the cost of missing fuel at local pump price.

## 5. Mileage

Economy, Compact, and SUV categories include unlimited mileage. Luxury, Sports, and Convertible categories include 200 miles/day, with excess mileage billed at $0.45/mile.

## 6. Prohibited Use

Vehicles may not be used for: commercial ride-sharing or delivery services, off-road driving, towing, or travel outside the country of rental without prior written approval from Avelto Rentals. Violation of these terms voids insurance coverage described in the Insurance & Damage Waiver Policy.

## 7. Return Condition

Vehicles must be returned in the condition recorded at pickup, ordinary wear excepted. Excessive interior soiling (beyond normal use) incurs a cleaning fee. Smoking in any vehicle is prohibited and incurs a deep-cleaning fee.

## 8. Governing Terms

This agreement is supplemented by the Insurance & Damage Waiver Policy, the Cancellation & Refund Policy, and any country-specific driving rules applicable to the pickup country, all of which form part of the full rental contract.
