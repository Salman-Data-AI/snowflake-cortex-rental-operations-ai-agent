# Avelto Rentals — Roadside Assistance Guide

**Document type:** Customer-facing guide
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## When to Contact Roadside Assistance
Contact Roadside Assistance for: flat tires, dead batteries, lockouts, mechanical breakdowns, or being stranded due to a vehicle fault. Roadside Assistance is included at no charge for issues not caused by customer misuse, as described in the Insurance & Damage Waiver Policy.

## What to Do
1. Move the vehicle to a safe location if possible; enable hazard lights.
2. Contact Support via phone, chat, or email with your reservation number and current location.
3. Do not attempt repairs yourself; unauthorized repairs may void coverage.
4. Stay with the vehicle unless it is unsafe to do so.

## Response Times
Standard response time is 60–90 minutes in metro areas covered by our 10 branches, and up to 3 hours in more remote pickup/return locations. Platinum-tier Avelto Miles members receive priority dispatch.

## Replacement Vehicles
If a breakdown cannot be resolved roadside, Avelto Rentals will arrange a replacement vehicle in the same category where available, or the next closest category with a partial refund of the daily rate difference. Replacement vehicle delivery is prioritized from the nearest branch that stocks the customer's category — check the Driving Rules by Region and branch category availability before promising a same-category replacement outside the Convertible pilot branches (Adamstad, Andersonshire, North Valerie), since Convertible units are not stocked elsewhere.

## Towing
Towing to the nearest Avelto branch or approved partner garage is included. Towing to a location of the customer's choosing beyond the nearest covered option is billed at cost.

## After-Hours Support
Roadside Assistance is available 24/7 via phone. Chat and email support for roadside issues are monitored with reduced coverage between midnight and 6 a.m. local branch time; phone remains the fastest channel overnight.
