# Avelto Rentals — Cancellation & Refund Policy

**Document type:** Customer-facing policy
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

## Standard Cancellations
Reservations cancelled more than 48 hours before the scheduled pickup time receive a full refund to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Reservations cancelled between 48 and 24 hours before pickup receive a 50% refund. Reservations cancelled less than 24 hours before pickup, or no-shows, are non-refundable.

## Category-Specific Notes
Sports and Convertible category bookings require a non-refundable $50 booking deposit at reservation time, applied toward the final rental cost. This deposit is forfeited on any cancellation, regardless of timing, due to the limited fleet size in these categories.

## Modifications
Changing pickup/return dates or branch does not count as a cancellation and does not trigger cancellation fees, provided the change is made more than 24 hours before the original pickup time and the new dates are confirmed as available.

## Early Returns
Returning a vehicle before the agreed end date does not generate a partial refund for the unused days, except for rentals of 7 days or longer, which are eligible for a pro-rated refund of unused full days beyond the 7-day mark.

## Refunds for Service Issues
If Avelto Rentals cannot provide the reserved category at pickup and no comparable upgrade is available, the customer receives a full refund plus a 1,000-point Avelto Miles credit, regardless of cancellation timing rules above.

## How to Request a Refund
Refund requests should be submitted through Support with the reservation number and reason for the request. Most refund decisions are communicated within 3 business days of the request.
