# Avelto Rentals — Driving Rules by Region

**Document type:** Customer-facing reference
**Applies to:** Branches by country
**Last updated:** 2026-01-01

This document summarizes country-specific rules that apply on top of the standard Rental Agreement Terms, based on the country of the pickup branch.

## United States (branches: South Eric, Jordanville, Suttonburgh)
Minimum liability coverage follows state law at the pickup branch's location. Renters must be 21+; renters 21–24 pay the Young Driver Surcharge. Toll transponders are provided in-vehicle; unpaid tolls are billed to the card on file plus a $15 administrative fee per toll event.

## Canada (branch: West Kristinaside)
Winter tires are mandatory on all vehicles from December 1 through March 31 and are fitted automatically at no extra cost during that window. Renters must hold a license valid for at least 12 months, consistent with the standard policy.

## Germany (branches: West Robinview, Adamstad)
Vehicles may be driven on the Autobahn without a posted speed limit in unrestricted sections; Sports and Convertible category renters are advised that CDW deductibles still apply in full regardless of posted limits. An international driving permit is required for renters whose license was not issued in the EU, UK, or a small set of recognized countries.

## France (branch: Davidstad)
All vehicles are equipped with a reflective safety vest and warning triangle as required by French law; do not remove these from the vehicle. Low-emission zone (ZFE) restrictions apply in some city centers — check the in-vehicle signage decal for the vehicle's emissions class before entering a restricted zone.

## United Kingdom (branch: Andersonshire)
Driving is on the left. Renters from countries where driving is on the right should request the orientation briefing offered free at pickup at this branch. The Convertible fleet pilot is active at this branch.

## Japan (branch: Juarezview)
An International Driving Permit (1949 Geneva Convention format) is required for all non-Japanese-licensed renters; Avelto Rentals cannot rent to renters holding only a Japan-incompatible IDP format. Expressway tolls are charged automatically via the vehicle's ETC card, provided in-vehicle.

## Australia (branch: North Valerie)
Renters must be 21+ with 12 months' licensed driving history. Unsealed/dirt-road driving is prohibited under the standard CDW and voids coverage per the Insurance & Damage Waiver Policy. The Convertible fleet pilot is active at this branch.
